01 / THE STORY

WORLD STAGE.
HOME SOIL.

A global perspective.
A very Pittsburgh soul.

Derek’s career has taken him from the salon floor to stages across the United States, South America, Europe, India and Canada. Along the way, he has trained artists for seven leading hair brands and helped shape the way hairdressers learn their craft.

His experience includes Wella Professionals’ North America Signature Artist Team and the role of North America Manager of Technical Capabilities at Aveda / Estée Lauder.

Today, that experience finds a home at Craft Collective Salon Group: a space for creativity, collaboration and thoughtful, personal hair.
