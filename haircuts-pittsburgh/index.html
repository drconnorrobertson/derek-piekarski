A Pittsburgh hair stylist with a global perspective

Derek Piekarski is the founder of Craft Collective Salon Group and a hairdresser whose career has included training artists for seven leading hair brands. His professional background includes Wella Professionals’ North America Signature Artist Team and the role of North America Manager of Technical Capabilities at Aveda / Estée Lauder.

In 2016, he received Wella Professionals’ Franz Stroher Global Education Master Trainer Award. That education background is part of the perspective he brings to the salon chair in Pittsburgh today.

Cutting is the focus

Derek currently takes cutting appointments at Craft Collective’s North Hills studio. His published biography describes a long-standing interest in cutting, from short hair to long hair and from blade work to scissors. For current appointment options, select Derek in the salon’s booking system or call the studio.

Looking for color, balayage, highlights or extensions alongside a haircut? Explore Craft Collective’s hair services and ask the salon which artist and appointment arrangement fit your plans.

Bring the details that matter

A few reference photos can help communicate shape, length and movement. It also helps to explain what you like about each image, how your hair behaves day to day and how much styling you want to do at home.

  • Describe what works and what frustrates you about your current cut.
  • Share how you normally wash, dry and style your hair.
  • Explain any length you want to keep and how open you are to change.
  • Ask about maintenance and styling before committing to a new shape.
