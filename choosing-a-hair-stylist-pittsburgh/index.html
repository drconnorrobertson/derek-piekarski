Start with the appointment, not the superlative

When you search for the best hair salon in Pittsburgh, you may be looking for very different things: a precise short cut, a new long shape, a color correction or a comfortable place to ask for a change. The strongest choice is the stylist whose experience and approach fit your specific appointment.

Use “best” as the start of your search. Then compare the details that affect the result and your experience.

Look for relevant work

Review a stylist’s portfolio for shapes, lengths and textures that relate to your hair and goals. Pay attention to the cut itself as well as the finish. A carefully styled photograph can be inspiring, but your day-to-day result also depends on your hair, routine and maintenance.

When browsing a salon gallery, check whether the work is credited to an individual artist or represents the whole team. The gallery on Derek’s homepage includes Craft Collective work and is labeled accordingly.

Ask about the conversation before the cut

A useful consultation covers the result you want, what your hair is doing now and how you want to care for it afterward. Bring references, explain your styling routine and ask which parts of an inspiration photo are realistic for your hair.

  • Does this cut work with my usual styling routine?
  • What length or shape would you adjust for my hair?
  • How often would I need to maintain this look?
  • What appointment should I book, and what does it include?

Match the service to the artist

Education and experience give useful context, but they should connect to the service you need. Derek Piekarski’s background includes Wella Professionals and Aveda / Estée Lauder technical education roles. He currently takes cutting appointments at Craft Collective’s North Hills location.

If your main goal is balayage, highlights, blonding or another color service, explore Craft Collective’s service pages and artist team to find the appropriate appointment.

Make the practical details work

Confirm the location, availability, price and any consultation requirements before booking. A salon you can return to consistently is often a better fit for a look that needs regular upkeep.
